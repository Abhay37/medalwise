Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Medalwise provides live one-to-one Olympiad coaching online. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices families have. Questions go to hello@medalwise.com.

What we collect

  • From the trial form: the parent's name and email, the student's first name, country, grade, target contest, time zone, preferred lesson times, plan of interest, and any note you add.
  • During coaching: lesson recordings, shared whiteboard work, homework, practice scores and messages between the family and the coach.
  • For payment: handled by our payment provider. We see the payment status and the last digits of the card, never the full card number.

We do not ask for a student's surname, school, address, photo or date of birth, and we do not use advertising trackers on this site.

Children under 13

Our students are often under 13. We collect a child's information only from and with the consent of a parent or guardian, which is why the trial form asks for the parent's email. A parent can ask at any time to see, correct or delete their child's information, or to stop further collection, by emailing us. We follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Canada's PIPEDA.

How we use it

  • To reply to trial requests, match the student with a coach and schedule lessons.
  • To run lessons, share progress reports with the parent and keep recordings so the student can rewatch them.
  • To take payment and keep the records the law requires.

We never sell personal information and never use it to advertise to children.

Who we share it with

Only with the services we need to run lessons: our form provider (FormSubmit), email, video calling and whiteboard tools, scheduling, and our payment provider. Each gets only what its job needs. We disclose information to authorities only when the law requires it or to protect a child's safety.

How long we keep it

  • Lesson recordings: 60 days, then deleted.
  • Trial requests that do not become lessons: 12 months.
  • Student records: while the student is active and 12 months after the last lesson, except payment records we must keep for tax purposes.

Coaches and safety

Coaches are background checked, every lesson is recorded and parents may join any lesson. Coaches contact families only through Medalwise channels, never on personal accounts.

Your choices

Email hello@medalwise.com to access, correct, export or delete your family's information. We reply within 30 days. Residents of California and other states with privacy laws have these same rights.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects how a child's information is used, we will email parents before the change applies.
