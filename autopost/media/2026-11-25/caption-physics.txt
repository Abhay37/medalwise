Tilt the axes along the slope. Perpendicular: u = 10 m/s, g = 5√3, so T = 4/√3 s. Along the slope: u = 10√3, g = −5, so d = 40 − 40/3 = 80/3 ≈ 26.7 m. The flat-ground formula gives 34.6 m, which is wrong here.
