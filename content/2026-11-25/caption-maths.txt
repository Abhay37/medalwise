Let t = xy. The expression becomes t + 2/t − 2, and x + y = 1 forces t ≤ 1/4, where t + 2/t is still decreasing. So the minimum is at t = 1/4: 1/4 + 8 − 2 = 25/4. AM-GM's 4 needs x = y = 1, which is impossible here.
