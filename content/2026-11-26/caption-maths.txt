A circle touching both axes in the first quadrant has centre (r, r). Through (2, 1): (2 − r)² + (1 − r)² = r², so r² − 6r + 5 = 0, giving r = 1 or r = 5. Both circles work: 1 + 5 = 6. The big one is easy to miss!
