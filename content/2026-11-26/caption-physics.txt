✅ Last answer: D, 62.5 Hz.
The wall hears 500 × 340/320 = 531.25 Hz and reflects it. The driver, moving towards the wall, hears 531.25 × 360/340 = 562.5 Hz. Beats = 562.5 − 500 = 62.5 Hz. The 2uf/v shortcut (58.8 Hz) is only approximate.
