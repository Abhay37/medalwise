Acid 10 mmol + NaOH 5 mmol leaves a buffer of 5 mmol CH₃COOH and 5 mmol CH₃COO⁻. HCl (0.5 mmol) turns acetate into acid: 4.5 and 5.5 mmol. pH = 4.74 + log(4.5/5.5) = 4.74 − 0.087 ≈ 4.65.
