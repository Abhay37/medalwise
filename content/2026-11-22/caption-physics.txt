✅ Last answer: A, mgR/12.
In a circular orbit E = −GMm/2r = −mgR²/2r. E(2R) = −mgR/4, E(3R) = −mgR/6. Energy needed = mgR/4 − mgR/6 = mgR/12. The PE rises by mgR/6, but the satellite gives back mgR/12 of KE.
