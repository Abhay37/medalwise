✅ Last answer: C, Ag₂CrO₄ in water, AgCl in 0.01 M AgNO₃.
Water: AgCl s ≈ 1.3 × 10⁻⁵ M, Ag₂CrO₄ s ≈ 6.5 × 10⁻⁵ M. With 0.01 M Ag⁺: AgCl s = 1.8 × 10⁻⁸ M, Ag₂CrO₄ s = Ksp/[Ag⁺]² = 1.1 × 10⁻⁸ M. The squared [Ag⁺] flips the order.
