✅ Last answer: C, 30 mL.
KMnO₄ oxidises BOTH parts of FeC₂O₄: Fe²⁺ → Fe³⁺ (1 e⁻) and C₂O₄²⁻ → 2CO₂ (2 e⁻), so n = 3. 10 × 0.1 × 3 = 3 mmol e⁻; MnO₄⁻ takes 5 e⁻ → 0.6 mmol → 0.6 / 0.02 = 30 mL.
