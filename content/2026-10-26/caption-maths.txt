✅ Last answer: 17 ordered pairs.
2026(x + y) = xy → (x − 2026)(y − 2026) = 2026² = 2²·1013², which has 9 positive divisors. 9 positive + 9 negative pairs, minus the one that gives x = y = 0: 9 + 9 − 1 = 17.
