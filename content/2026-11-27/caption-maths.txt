✅ Last answer: 309.
Inclusion-exclusion over the 5 banned pairs (12, 23, 34, 45, 56). If k pairs are forced, glue them into blocks: (6 − k)! ways. Total = 720 − 5·120 + 10·24 − 10·6 + 5·2 − 1 = 309. Not derangements (265)!
