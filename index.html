    How it works

    From first call to weekly lessons in four steps.

    1. Free 30-minute trial

      Your child solves a few problems with a coach. You get an honest read on their level.

    2. Matched coach and plan

      We pair them with a coach for their subject and exam, and send a season plan with target scores.

    3. Weekly live lessons

      Video call with a shared whiteboard. Every lesson is recorded so they can rewatch it.

    4. Monthly parent report

      Topics covered, mock scores, homework done and what comes next.

    Questions parents ask

    FAQ

    Which subjects and exams do you coach?

    Maths, physics, chemistry, biology, astronomy and computing Olympiads in every country we list, plus JEE Main, JEE Advanced and NEET for students in India and the Gulf. Pick your country at the top of the page to see its exams.

    Our coaches are in India. How does that work with our time zone?

    Every slot you see is shown in your local time.

    What happens in the free trial?

    A 30-minute video call. The coach asks your child to try a few contest problems, talks through their thinking, and tells you which level and tier fit. No card needed.

    What do we need for lessons?

    A laptop or tablet with a camera and a stable internet connection. A tablet with a stylus helps for writing on the shared whiteboard, but it is not required.

    Is the coaching only for students who are already strong?

    No. Many students start with their first school-level contest. The Foundation tier is built for them.

    Can we switch coaches?

    Yes, at any time and at no cost. Tell us and we will suggest another coach for the same exam.

    How do you keep students safe online?

    All lessons are recorded and kept for 60 days, coaches are background checked, and parents can join any lesson. Coaches never contact students on personal accounts.

    Are you connected to the contest organizers?

    No. Medalwise is an independent coaching service and is not affiliated with the MAA, USACO, CEMC, the CMS, HBCSE, IAPT, NTA, UKMT, the Australian Mathematics Trust or any other organizer.
