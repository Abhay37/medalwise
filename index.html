Problem of the week

AMC 10 level

For how many positive integers n ≤ 100 is n² + n + 1 divisible by 7?

Answer: (D) 29. Check residues mod 7: only n ≡ 2 (4+2+1 = 7) and n ≡ 4 (16+4+1 = 21) work. Up to 100 there are 15 numbers ≡ 2 and 14 numbers ≡ 4, so 29 in total.

Questions parents ask

FAQ

Our coaches are in India. How does that work with our time zone?

Coaches teach early-morning shifts in India, which lines up with weekday evenings and weekend mornings in North America. Every slot you see on the booking page is shown in your local time.

What happens in the free trial?

A 30-minute video call. The coach asks your child to try a few contest problems, talks through their thinking, and tells you which level and tier fit. No card needed.

What do we need for lessons?

A laptop or tablet with a camera and a stable internet connection. A tablet with a stylus helps for writing on the shared whiteboard, but it is not required.

Is the coaching only for students who are already strong?

No. Many students start with AMC 8 or MATHCOUNTS with no contest experience. The Foundation tier is built for them.

Can we switch coaches?

Yes, at any time and at no cost. Tell us and we will suggest another coach for the same exam.

How do you keep students safe online?

All lessons are recorded and kept for 60 days, coaches are background checked, and parents can join any lesson. Coaches never contact students on personal accounts.

Are you connected to the contest organizers?

No. Medalwise is an independent coaching service and is not affiliated with the MAA, USACO, CEMC, the CMS or any other organizer.
