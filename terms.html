The service

Medalwise matches students with a coach for live one-to-one online lessons that prepare them for math, computing and science contests. Lessons are 60 minutes unless agreed otherwise. Medalwise is not affiliated with MAA, MATHCOUNTS, USACO, AAPT, ACS, USABO, the University of Waterloo CEMC, the CMS or any other contest organizer, and we cannot promise any particular score, qualification or award.

Free trial

The first 30-minute lesson is free, with no payment details needed. We reply to trial requests within one working day.

Prices and payment

  • Foundation US$39 / C$54, Contender US$59 / C$82, Elite US$99 / C$137 per 60-minute lesson.
  • A pack of 8 lessons saves 10%.
  • Single lessons are paid before the lesson; packs are paid up front. Prices for an active pack do not change during that pack.

Rescheduling and missed lessons

  • Move a lesson for free up to 12 hours before it starts.
  • A lesson cancelled with less than 12 hours' notice, or missed, counts as taken.
  • If the coach cancels or misses a lesson, you get a free replacement or a refund for it, your choice.

Refunds

  • There is no contract. You can stop at any time.
  • Unused lessons in a pack are refunded in full if you stop.
  • If a lesson was not up to standard, tell us within 7 days and we will refund it or rebook it with another coach.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 working days.

Recordings and safety

Every lesson is recorded and kept for 60 days for the student to rewatch and for safeguarding. Parents may join any lesson. Students and parents agree not to contact coaches outside Medalwise channels or to hire a Medalwise coach privately for 12 months after their last lesson.

Conduct

We expect respectful behaviour from students, parents and coaches. We may end coaching after a warning if a lesson becomes unsafe or abusive, and refund any unused lessons.

Contest rules

Coaches will not help with any contest while it is being written and will not share problems that are still under embargo. Families agree to follow each contest's own rules.

Liability

Our total liability for any claim is limited to the amount paid for the lessons in question. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under consumer law where you live.

Changes

We will email families at least 14 days before any change to prices or these terms applies to them.
